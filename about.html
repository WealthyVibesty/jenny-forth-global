About Jenny Forth

She started over. Then she built a legacy.

Your guide has walked this road

Jenny Forth built her life and her businesses after starting over. She knows what it costs to begin again, and she turned that beginning into a global brand. Today she works across luxury real estate and investment, international speaking, coaching and mentorship, and publishing.

She also founded Jenny's Human Rights Foundation, which supports survivors of domestic violence and human trafficking, because a legacy that only helps one person is not a legacy at all.

"Wealth is not measured by how much you can buy. It is measured by how much of your life you own."
"Confidence doesn't come before action. Confidence comes from action."

That is the path she walks you down: act, own, and build a legacy.

The mission

You get a trustworthy path to personal and financial growth. Jenny weaves coaching, real estate, and inspirational media into a single premium experience, so your wealth and your authority grow together instead of competing for your time.

If you earn well but own little of your own life, if your money sits still while another year passes, you are exactly who this brand was built for.
